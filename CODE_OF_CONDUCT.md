# bot.Box Code of Conduct

Everyone is welcome to participate in bot.Box. Treat people with respect across
backgrounds, identities, abilities, and levels of experience. Discuss the work
on its merits, give specific feedback, and make room for people to ask questions
and disagree in good faith.

Harassment, discrimination, slurs, personal attacks, threats, unwanted sexual
attention, and sharing someone's private information without consent are
unacceptable. Do not retaliate against someone for reporting an incident or
helping resolve it.

This code applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests,
discussions, and project events. It also applies when someone officially
represents bot.Box in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report incidents privately to the project maintainer, Jan Gehring, at
<jan@dunkel.tech>. If you can, include what happened and links or timestamps
that help identify the incident. The maintainer will limit disclosure to what
is necessary to investigate and respond or what the law requires.

The maintainer may remove content, issue a warning, restrict participation, or
ban someone from project spaces. The response should reflect the severity and
frequency of the conduct and its impact on the people involved.
